Christina Perng

I am a real estate agent at Compass Menlo Park. Having lived in the Bay Area for 23 years and currently residing in Palo Alto, I have personally experienced every change this land has seen — from the rise of the housing market to the growth of communities. This familiarity allows me to help clients better assess a neighborhood, a street, or even a single home.

Over 15 years, I have specialized in residential real estate across Palo Alto, Menlo Park, and cities throughout the Bay Area, having assisted over 200 families. I approach every client with the same care and expertise, accompanying them every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions 常見問題

常見問題解答
Q

How competitive is the Bay Area market?

現在灣區市場競爭激烈嗎？

The Bay Area remains one of the most competitive markets in the US. Well-priced homes often receive multiple offers. I help buyers prepare strategically and sellers position for strong offers.

Q

Do I need pre-approval before looking at homes?

看房前需要先取得貸款預批嗎？

Yes — strongly recommended. Bay Area sellers expect buyers to be pre-approved. It also helps you know your budget and move quickly when you find the right home.

Q

How long does it take to buy a home?

買房通常需要多長時間？

Closing typically takes 21–30 days after offer acceptance. The house-hunting phase varies — some clients find their home in weeks, others in a few months.

Q

Can you help Chinese-speaking clients?

您可以全程用中文服務嗎？

Absolutely. I am fully bilingual in Chinese and English and provide complete service in both languages from initial consultation through closing. 我提供全程中英文雙語服務。
